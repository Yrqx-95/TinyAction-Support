Support

How can we help?

tiny action works offline and keeps your actions and wins on your device.

Getting started

What happens when I open the app?

You immediately get one small action. Tap “I did it” when you finish, “Make it smaller” for an easier version, or “Something else” to see another idea.

How do I change modes?

Tap the current mode near the top of the home screen. Choose Everyday, Two of us, Friends, or Solo weekend. Each mode has its own actions, categories, color, and history count.

Make it fit the moment

In Everyday mode you can limit time, stay at home, stay indoors, or stay in place. Other modes offer categories and session length that match the people and situation.

If no built-in action fits strict filters, change a filter, choose another category, open a favorite, or add your own action.

Wins and custom actions

Tap the win counter to review recent actions, see totals for each mode, filter the list, and share a win. You can clear history from this screen. Favorites, ratings, custom actions, and wins are stored on your device.

Language, appearance, and feedback

Tap the gear to switch among English, Japanese, and Traditional Chinese; choose System, Light, or Dark appearance; and change animation or haptic feedback.

Data and reinstalling

tiny action has no account or developer-operated cloud sync. Removing the app removes its local data from the device. Apple-managed device backups may include app data depending on your device settings.
