Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Overview

tiny action does not collect or send personal information to the developer. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking software.

Information stored on your device

Your preferences, favorites, ratings, custom actions, custom guided paths, saved moments, purchase entitlement, and completed-action history are stored locally on your device. Your Apple device backup settings may include this information in a backup managed by Apple.

Purchases

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple through StoreKit. The app checks Apple-signed transaction information and caches the entitlement on your device so unlocked tools can remain available offline. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Sharing

If you choose to share a small win or a built-in guided path, tiny action creates a share card and opens Apple's system share sheet. You choose the destination. Any service you select handles the shared content under its own privacy policy.

Deleting information

You can clear completed-action history inside the app. Removing the app removes its local container from the device. Previously created device backups are managed separately through your Apple settings.

Support messages

If you contact support, the email service you use may process your message and contact details. The developer uses the information you provide only to respond to your request.

Children

tiny action is not specifically directed to children, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information through the app.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will show its revision date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to quanxing_apple@icloud.com. For app help, visit Support.
